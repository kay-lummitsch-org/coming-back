COMING NEXT: THE DIGITAL JOURNEYMEN – THE AGENTS EDITION

Almost ten years ago, I wrote a small book. THE DIGITAL JOURNEYMEN. Damn good digital advice. Before that came three books on the Swisscom API Journey. They grew out of building Swisscom's API program – and out of many conversations with people who wanted to change their organizations.

The technology has moved on. The people questions are still with us.

And now there's a new one: what happens when it's not only people who get stuck in our organizations – but our agents, too?

AI can only act where your organization lets it. Through APIs. And anyone who has lived through an API program knows: technology was rarely the problem. Power lines. Silos – vertical and horizontal. “We already have that.” And the old divide between IT and business has often proved remarkably stubborn.

The agents will run into exactly the same walls.

Actually, I only wanted to write one book. PEOPLE FIRST — WTF! About people. About what we put in their way. I was done with APIs. Or so I thought.

Then they caught up with me again.

So much for that. Now it's two.
PEOPLE FIRST — WTF! for the people.
THE DIGITAL JOURNEYMEN – The Agents Edition for APIs in the age of AI.

Short chapters. Real experience. Very little patience for the status quo.

The original is free to read on this website. PEOPLE FIRST — WTF! comes first. The Agents Edition follows. The covers are done.
