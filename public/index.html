HOW WE RUN THE BUSINESS

Build the conditions
for a damn
good job.

01

Make good work possible.

Find the barriers with the people closest to the work. Change the decisions, structures and habits that keep those barriers in place.

02

Give people room to act.

Put information, tools and authority where they can make a difference. Let speaking up lead to visible action.

03

Keep the promises we sell.

Bring customer promises, resources and the reality of delivery together. Then check whether the changes actually help.
