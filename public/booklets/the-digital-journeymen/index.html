WHY IT STILL MATTERS TO ME

There’s nothing
but people.

I wrote this book after years of working on an API program and talking to people trying to change their organizations.

We had tools. We had strategies. We had very convincing explanations for why real change would have to wait.

So I put my questions into a small book. Corporate DNA. Resistance. The status quo. And a little Pippi Longstocking.

Reading it today, I recognise the questions that still keep me busy.
