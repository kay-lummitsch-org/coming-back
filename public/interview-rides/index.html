SWITZERLAND. A MOTORCYCLE. A GOOD CONVERSATION.

People.
And their stories.

From the next motorcycle season, I’d like to invite people on a shared ride through Switzerland.

I’m curious about people and what moves them. What they’ve experienced. What they believe in. What’s on their minds. We can talk about transformation. Or about life. The two are probably difficult to separate anyway.

Ride with me as a passenger, bring your own bike – or we’ll rent you the motorcycle you’ve always wanted to try.

We’ll explore your dream bike together, subject to availability and the appropriate riding licence.

We’ll record our conversation – on the road and over a coffee. A camera vehicle will accompany us, and we’ll have GoPros and Insta360 cameras on board.

THIS IS GOING TO BE GOOD – I CAN HARDLY WAIT!
